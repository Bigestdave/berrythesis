THE LONG-TERM CASE

NEAR is a settlement business.

The investable question is whether the slice NEAR keeps from its Intents rail gets valued as recurring settlement revenue — the reclassification that re-rated Visa, LSEG, and ICE. Today's price assumes a lot, so the position is conditional.

THE EVIDENCE FOR

Why the rail could compound

• $11.22B verified stablecoin settlement floor into intents.near across nine confirmed 6-decimal USD contracts (29.3% of the $38.26B NEAR reports). • Price-free rail activity: stable settlements per day stepped up from 4,600 (Jun-Jul 26) to 7,357 (Aug), 7,682 (Sep), 7,852 (Oct) and 8,622/day (Nov 1-11). • Growing share of L1 activity: intents.near received 32,765 txs/day in Mar-26 and 64,962 in Oct-26 (122,954 in Nov 1-11), rising from 0.9% to 8.9% to 17.0% of all L1 transactions. Median caller count: 147/day (solvers and relayers, not end users). • Protocol capture climbing: retained take expanded 11.5% → 16.4% → ~28–30% (retained take ~3–5 bps on ~15.9 bps gross fees; cumulative net revenue ~$11.7M on ~$61M gross). • Historical comparables (Visa/Mastercard 30 bps on zero credit risk, LSEG data re-rating, ICE clearing, AWS SOTP) show markets pay the highest multiples for recurring, asset-light tolls without credit risk.
Verified on-chain floor · BigQuery ft_events
WHAT HAS TO BE TRUE

Conditions and open risks

• No detectable lead from flow to network usage (permutation p = 0.44 to 0.75) or to token price (monthly lead r = -0.24, p=0.31; weekly +0.01, p=0.89). Sample sizes are small (~22 months, ~94 weeks), so this fails to detect a lead rather than proving absence. • The L1 is shrinking: daily active accounts down -74% YoY (72k Oct-26 vs 273k Oct-25, -92% vs Jan-25 882k). The settlement rail carries the thesis with no second engine. • Returns are concentrated: +251% since fee switch at 108% annualised volatility (Sharpe 1.62), but one month (Sep 2026) supplies 81% of the gain and Sharpe without it is 0.36. Max drawdown -43.6%. • Moat is unproven: intent routing is commoditised today (Across ran 10-15 bps with $0 capture). NEAR has Visa's take rate, not Visa's defensibility. • Valuation needs a lot to be true: at 151× run-rate sales ($4.52B), a 15× multiple needs $301M revenue (10× today). At 50× and 30 bps it needs ~$30B (1.8× verified stable run-rate). • Every link after verified volume is reported, not independently verified. The retained take is the number to watch.

The single falsifier (§10)

“If net revenue (not volume) fails to grow ≥ 50% over the next four quarters, the 'usage reaches the token' thesis is dead regardless of what volume does.”

1. Net revenue (not volume) fails to grow ≥ 50% over four quarters (kills thesis). 2. Protocol capture falls below 15% for two consecutive months: abandon. 3. Stable settlement $/day below own 6-month average for two consecutive months: exit. 4. Valuation trigger: P/S ≤ 100x (≤ $2.29 at today's revenue, or ≥ $45M/yr revenue) before accumulating. 5. Growth trigger: capture ≥ 25% for two months AND stable $/day at 3-month high.

Permutation Lead-Lag Testing: No Detectable Lead From Flow

10,000-shuffle permutation tests on BigQuery transactions find no detectable lead from flow growth to active accounts (monthly lag+1 r=+0.10, p=0.69; weekly p=0.44) or transactions (monthly p=0.70; weekly p=0.75). Price does no better at leading usage (smallest p=0.25). On Binance daily closes, monthly flow-vs-price lead r=-0.24 (p=0.31) and weekly r=+0.01 (p=0.89). Sample sizes are small (~22 months, ~94 weeks), so this fails to detect a lead rather than proving absence.
